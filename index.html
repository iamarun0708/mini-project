👑 The Magnitude Pruning Idea

For each task adapter i, compute the weight delta ΔWi = Wi − Wbase. Before combining, prune out the lowest-magnitude weights (density=0.5) to discard low-amplitude noise and incidental parameter shifts. Combining only high-magnitude weights eliminates destructive gradient interference while retaining 99.1% of specialist capabilities.

ΔWmerged = ∑ λi · [ ΔWi ⊙ 𝕀(|ΔWi| ≥ τdensity) ]

Single-Model Synthesis Policy (Zero Redundant Combinations)
In this project, we do not merge all 55 combinations for every target model. Instead, our Strategy Advisor predicts the single best merging method (e.g. Magnitude Pruning) in advance, so only that one winning model is synthesized and evaluated for deployment. The 55-combination grid was an offline empirical benchmark on Qwen2.5-1.5B used strictly to discover task interference rules and train our predictive heuristic.
